GPT 生成广告首帧

开一个新的 GPT 对话，上传你的照片，贴上这段：

黑底杯子首帧
Use the uploaded photo as the exact reference for the drink. Keep the cup identical: clear plastic cup, frosted dome-less flat lid with ridged rim, bright yellow straw, sky-blue milk tea, black tapioca pearls at the bottom, and the pink plastic bag tied around the cup exactly as in the photo. Do not upgrade, clean, or redesign anything — it must still look like a cheap night-market drink.

New setting: a luxury perfume-commercial studio. Seamless pure black background, the cup standing alone on a glossy black surface with a faint reflection.

Lighting (one fixed setup): a single narrow strip softbox as key light from the upper left at about 45 degrees, neutral-cool daylight around 5600K, lighting the left side of the cup; the right side falls into deep shadow. One thin rim light from behind on the right, outlining the cup edge and the pink bag. No other light sources.

Real photo texture: uneven condensation droplets on the cup (different sizes, some running), small scratches on the plastic, pearls slightly different in size and partly clumped, blue drink slightly uneven in color near the bottom.

Shot: vertical 9:16, low angle, 100mm macro product shot, shallow depth of field, cup fills two-thirds of the frame. No text, no logo, no watermark.

重点是杯子、塑胶袋、吸管一样都不能变。便宜的杯子配上奢侈品的光，笑点就在这里。你的饮料不一样的话，把第一段的描述换成你的杯子就好。

再开一个新对话，上传你的原图和刚生成的首帧，生成一张珍珠特写：

珍珠落下特写
Image 1 is the color reference for the drink: match this exact sky-blue milk tea color and these black tapioca pearls. Image 2 is the lighting reference: match its black studio and light exactly.

Create a high-end beverage commercial macro photo, vertical 9:16.

Scene: extreme close-up at liquid level of a perfectly still surface of sky-blue milk tea filling the lower third of the frame. Four black tapioca pearls are frozen in mid-air, falling toward the surface: the lowest one is about to touch the liquid, the others are higher at staggered heights. Nothing has hit the surface yet — no splash, no ripples.

Lighting (one fixed setup, same as Image 2): pure black background. Cool-white key light from the front-left lights the pearls' left sides and the front of the liquid surface. A thin rim light from behind on the right gives each pearl a bright edge highlight. The right sides of the pearls fall into shadow. No other light sources.

Real photography texture, not CGI: pearls slightly different in size and shape, glossy with a sticky syrup sheen and a few matte spots; the milk tea surface has tiny bubbles and a slightly uneven tone; very shallow depth of field, the farthest pearl slightly soft.

Camera: 100mm macro lens look, camera exactly at the liquid surface height. No cup visible, no text, no logo, no watermark.

15 秒多镜头广告
Image 1 is the hero shot and the look reference: the cup in a black studio — copy its framing for Shot 1 and its lighting for every shot. Image 2 is the reference for the real drink: a cheap night-market sky-blue milk tea in a ridged clear plastic cup, frosted flat lid, yellow straw, black tapioca pearls at the bottom, a pink plastic bag tied around the cup. Image 3 is the reference for the black tapioca pearls. In every shot the cup, pink bag, yellow straw and blue color stay exactly like the references — it must still look like a cheap plastic-bag drink. No text, no logo.

Lighting (fixed for every shot): pure black studio, cool-white key light from the front-left, a thin rim light from behind on the right outlining every edge, glossy black floor with reflections. No other light sources.

A fast, high-energy luxury beverage commercial with hard cuts, each shot about 2 seconds, escalating in scale:

Shot 1: the hero cup exactly as in Image 1; a sharp light sweep runs across the cup from left to right, the camera snaps forward toward it.
Shot 2: macro inside the drink — sky-blue milk tea swirling into a vortex, black pearls spiralling upward through it, slow motion.
Shot 3: black pearls burst upward out of a sky-blue milk tea splash and hang frozen in the air, ultra slow motion, the camera slowly orbits around them. Every splash and droplet is sky-blue milk tea, not white.
Shot 4: the pink plastic bag ripples in slow motion like luxury silk in the wind against black, its edges glowing from the rim light.
Shot 5: the yellow straw stabs down through the lid in one fast move, a small sky-blue splash bursts from the straw hole.
Shot 6: final hero shot — the cup lands on the glossy black floor, a ring of sky-blue splash bursts out around its base and falls back, low-angle camera slowly pushes in, the cup centered with empty black space above it for a title.

Audio: native sound effects only, no music, no voice — a deep whoosh on each cut, liquid swirls, a crisp straw stab, and a heavy bass impact when the cup lands.

我这支 15 秒花了 105 credit。第 3 镜特别写了“溅起来是蓝色，不是白色”，不写的话，模型很容易溅出白牛奶。

4

CapCut 剪到 10 秒

  • 剪短：漩涡那镜最拖，剪到 1 秒多
  • 变速：漩涡、塑胶袋、吸管加速 1.3–1.6 倍；珍珠爆起那镜保留慢动作
  • 转场：每次切镜头加“闪白”，约 3 帧
  • 冲击感：每镜开头用关键帧缩放 108% → 100%，约 0.2 秒
  • 音乐：搜 luxury 或 epic trailer，用“踩点”让最重的鼓点对准杯子落地
  • 结尾字：「色素，是一种回忆」
